# Business Summary

## Portfolio Overview

The analyzed portfolio contains 677,991 motor insurance policies with approximately 358,482.84 years of exposure and 26,444 observed claims.

Key portfolio indicators:

- Claim Frequency: 7.38%
- Total Claim Cost: €59.91M
- Average Claim Severity: €2,265.51

## Key Findings

### 1. Bonus-Malus 100+

The 100+ Bonus-Malus segment shows a substantially higher observed claim frequency (26.25%) and average severity (€4,948.36).

### 2. Drivers Under 25

Drivers under 25 show an observed claim frequency of 16.10% and average severity of approximately €5,839.

### 3. Claim Cost Concentration

The most expensive 1% of claims account for approximately 37.99% of total claim cost.

### 4. Regional Patterns

Rhone-Alpes has the highest observed regional claim frequency (9.34%), while Centre has the highest total claim cost (approximately €19.07M).

## Recommendations

### Recommendation 1 — Monitor Bonus-Malus 100+

**Evidence:** 26.25% claim frequency and €4,948 average severity.

**Action:** Prioritize this segment for regular claims monitoring and detailed portfolio review.

**KPI:** Claim Frequency and Average Claim Severity.

### Recommendation 2 — Monitor Drivers Under 25

**Evidence:** 16.10% claim frequency and approximately €5,839 average severity.

**Action:** Track this segment separately in recurring claims and portfolio reviews.

**KPI:** Claim Frequency and Average Claim Severity.

### Recommendation 3 — Focus on High-Cost Claims

**Evidence:** The top 1% of claims account for approximately 37.99% of total claim cost.

**Action:** Maintain focused monitoring of high-cost claims and their contribution to total claim expenditure.

**KPI:** Cumulative Claim Cost Share.

## Important Interpretation

These findings describe observed patterns in the historical dataset. They should not be interpreted as causal effects, pricing recommendations, or evidence that a specific characteristic causes higher insurance losses.
